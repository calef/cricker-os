# 127. The seL4 machine: a Jetson TX1, so identical silicon referees the comparison

**Status: NOT-STARTED.** Raised 2026-08-15 from the aarch64 board survey
(notes/aarch64-board-survey.md, which holds the candidate table, the sel4bench support tiers with
sources, and the port-cost analysis this block summarizes). **Purchased 2026-08-15 (calef): a
factory-sealed TX1 developer kit, $89.99, delivery estimated 2026-08-19 to -26.** The milestone
completes when the board has printed a byte over serial; the sel4bench comparison itself remains
milestone 25's, sequenced behind the customer path by the ranking function.

**Gate: HARDWARE.** The kit is in transit, estimated 2026-08-19 to -26; nothing else blocks.
The two pre-board lanes the block names are deliberately NOT gated and can run now.

**Why this board and no other.** The seL4 Foundation publishes aarch64 benchmark numbers for
exactly one platform, the TX1, and they are the 413/426-cycle figures notes/benchmarks.md already
compares against, regenerated by their CI within days of the survey. Owning that silicon upgrades
the thesis's seL4 clause from a caveated cross-machine comparison ("a large part of the gap
closing is the machine, not the kernel") to identical-die measurement, and referees our
methodology for free: our sel4bench run on this board can be validated against published figures
before we trust it against ourselves. The purchase is an option on the thesis's named claim, made
while EOL supply exists; the work waits its turn.

**Port cost, from the survey**: near the floor. GIC-400 is the GICv2 our driver speaks, the Tegra
UART is 16550-compatible in the reg-shift shape the JH7110 already taught `ns16550.rs`, the timer
is architected, boot is stock U-Boot from SD taking the Image header we already emit, PSCI comes
from upstream TF-A's tegra210 BL31. Two prerequisites are buildable before the box arrives, each
its own lane: the EL2 to EL1 entry drop in aarch64 boot.s (U-Boot enters at EL2; QEMU's ELF path
never did), and milestone 74's aarch64 PMCCNTR half, which is what the PMU is for.

**To verify at the bench** (the survey's unknowns, kept honest): kit contents against the retail
manifest; the flashed L4T revision and whether U-Boot comes up without a JetPack reflash detour;
entry EL and the DTB register from this U-Boot; PSCI visibility to a non-Linux payload on shipped
firmware; PMCCNTR readable at EL1; what pins the 1.9 GHz clock; sel4bench booting from SD rather
than the Foundation's rig; the A57 errata sheet negative-check.

## Scope note

This is the machine and its serial byte, nothing else; milestones 25 (the comparison) and 74 (the
counters) stay themselves. The survey's other finding is recorded where it belongs and not here:
`gic.rs` is GICv2-only, and a GICv3 driver milestone should be minted deliberately someday, since
this is the last GICv2 generation anyone will buy. Nothing here advances the aarch64 IOMMU story;
16b's silicon gate stands.
